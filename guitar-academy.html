Guitar & Music Theory — Made Simple

Learn the theory behind what you are playing without getting buried in confusing terminology. We break down notes, scales, keys, chords, intervals, and how songs are put together in a simple, practical way so you can understand it, hear it, and use it on the guitar.

Timing, Rhythm & Groove

Good playing starts with good timing. Students learn the basics of counting, steady rhythm, strumming patterns, note values, playing with a beat, and developing the internal sense of time that helps a guitarist stay locked in with a song, a band, or a worship team.

Practical Playing & Chord Progressions

We connect the lesson directly to real music. Learn chords, transitions, common chord progressions, fretboard movement, rhythm guitar, basic lead concepts, ear development, and how to take what you learn and actually use it in songs.

Songwriting & Creative Development

I also help students learn how to write their own songs — from chord progressions and song structure to melody, lyrics, worship songwriting, and turning an idea into a finished song.
